## Context

0014 made hand strength force the showdown knockdown alongside Posture, and left open how hand
strength converts and how much it weighs. The sim found the showdown decides about 3 in 4 hands,
and that this choice moves "better hand wins" and "skill beats a worse hand" more than anything
else. The review offered: A, Posture plus a fixed bonus per hand category; B, the better hand
wins unless far behind on Posture; C, Posture plus every card's points.

## Decision

Trey: "A". On presentation: "the fix is absolutely ordering ... each gets corresponding bonus ->
we see outcome", and the fall sized to the gap: "good thinking". On the size and on riders in the
same category: "20, simple".

- If no one is unhorsed after Pass 4, each rider's hand bonus is added to their Posture: 20 per
  step of hand category above high card.
- The rider lower on Posture after the bonus is knocked down, even above 0.
- Riders in the same category get the same bonus; Posture decides between them, and kickers break
  only an exact tie.
- The bonus is shown landing on the Posture bar before the knockdown, and the fall is sized to the
  gap.

## Consequences

- §2 win conditions 3–4, §4 *Tracks and showdown*, §8 and §11 updated. The §11 open question on
  the showdown is answered and removed; the §11 row "hand strength weight against Posture" becomes
  the hand bonus.
- The bonus steps run through the trick categories too (a flush is five steps). At the showdown
  those are mostly tricks that clashed, or a board trick both riders stand on.
- Within a rung, a stronger trick no longer outweighs Posture at the showdown; it breaks only an
  exact tie.
