## Context

0024 left a gap: a rider holds a trick, and a later card gives the board a trick that plays
over it, so none of their hole cards is in their best five. It is rare (a deal sim put it
around 0.02% of riders).

## Decision

Trey: "r3.3 recommended".

- Your hand is always your current best five cards. A held trick that a later board trick
  plays over is no longer yours.

## Consequences

- §6 *Ownership* updated.
- Under 0041 the rider isn't dropped down the ladder: they stand on the board's rung, which is
  as high or higher. What they lose is the unleash.
