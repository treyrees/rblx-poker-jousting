## Context

The straight flush is The Charge plus Suit Ascendant. Under 0031 both carry conditions. The
review asked whether the straight flush's hit stays unconditional or inherits them.

## Decision

Trey: "should inherit both conditions but be as strong as it needs".

- The straight flush's hit grows with the straight's meter and with the lean toward its suit's
  home.
- Played fully, it is the strongest hit on the ladder, sized so it beats every rung below,
  quads' ward included.

## Consequences

- §6 effects table updated. The royal flush follows the straight flush.
