## Context

§3: aim resolves in 16 half-step positions, and the dial "presents as snappy and granular with 8
labeled directions, not as 16 hard notches." The Magnet wheel springs into one notch per entry of
its cycle. Put to Trey: 16 spring notches with 8 labels; 8 spring notches with half steps reached
by holding between them; or 8 notches only.

## Decision

The wheel's cycle has 16 entries, so every aim position is a notch the needle beds into. The 8
directions get labels and dots; the half steps get small ticks. The Magnet constants are kept as
the tourney tuned them.

## Consequences

- Every §3 position is reachable by drag and flick.
- The constants were tuned on 8 notches a revolution; whether 16 feels too fine is a playtest
  question. The notch count is config.
