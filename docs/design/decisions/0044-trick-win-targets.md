## Context

§11 asked whether unleashed tricks win "about 75 to 90% of the time vs numeric hands", a band
from the old pillar 3. Under pillar 3 as it now reads (0002, 0018, 0025) a trick above the
opponent that unleashes wins virtually always.

## Decision

Trey: "r3.5 yes".

- A trick above the opponent, played to its design, wins overwhelmingly. The number is Trey's
  and is not set yet.
- A higher trick nearly always beats a lower one across rungs.
- The sim reports how often a held trick is drawn out, and splits trick win rates by whether the
  trick was played to its design (0031).
- The §11 intro asks "whether a trick above the opponent wins overwhelmingly" in place of
  "whether tricks win often but not always".

## Consequences

- §11 intro and open questions updated.
