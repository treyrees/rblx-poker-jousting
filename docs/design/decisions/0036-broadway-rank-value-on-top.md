## Context

§5 gives J, Q, K and A an effect "on top of their rank and suit". With per-card values (0034),
the session asked whether face cards carry their full rank value on top of the effect, or count
lower to pay for it.

## Decision

Trey: "on top, broadway should be strong".

- J, Q, K and A add their full rank value to their suit's stat, on top of their §5 effect.

## Consequences

- On the compressed curve a jack is 2.5 points and an ace 3. Jacks keep stat value although
  their §5 effect is information only, which a sim without readers values at zero.
- Whether "strong" wants faces stepped up above the linear curve is not decided.
