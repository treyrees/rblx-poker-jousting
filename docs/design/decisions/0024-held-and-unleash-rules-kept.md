## Context

The review recommended keeping these §6 rules unchanged and updating the frequency text to
owned-trick rates from a deal sim.

## Decision

Trey: "6.7 yes".

- Kept: ownership (a hole card must be part of the trick), riding as the best non-trick
  sub-hand, the +3/+4 held passives, once per hand, and auto-fire on Pass 4.
- The frequency text gives owned-trick rates: about 10% of riders by the river; first seen on
  the river 63%, turn 29%, flop 8%; about 18% of hands with a trick on at least one side, 2%
  on both; board alone 0.8%.

## Consequences

- §6 frequency text updated. The ownership gaps the review found (a trick lost when the
  river gives the board a higher trick; board-made tricks one rider improves on) go with the
  board-made round.
