## Context

0026 sized every trick's hit to unhorse on any pass. Trey then asked for the lower tricks to
interact with play: "Straights should incentivize the gameplay of not moving or fidgeting your
aim, with higher straights doing this much better ... Flushes achieve their gameplay by tilting
towards the suit they are in. FHs, quads, and straight flushes are rare enough to be fine as
designed."

## Decision

Trey, on the review's proposal to size hits "to unhorse when played to its design": "1 yes".

- A trick's hit is sized to unhorse from full Posture on any pass when the trick is played to
  its design.
- Straight: held (0032). Flush: aimed at its suit's home direction, full there, half on the
  diagonals beside it, nothing extra elsewhere.
- Full house and quads are unconditional. The straight flush inherits both conditions (0033).

## Consequences

- §6 *Trick effects on the unleash pass* and *Flush by suit* updated.
- The straight is about when you commit (hold); the flush about where you aim (lean). Both are
  public, so the out becomes the aim war: move to deny a return crit and lose power, or stay.
