## Context

0037 made a lean multiply the card points of the stats it points at. ♥ is Posture, a running
track: if a lean multiplied ♥ points directly, max Posture would change every time the rider
rotates. The session offered (a) the ♥ lean acts only at contact, for example by cutting the
Posture damage you take on that hit; (b) max Posture follows your aim; (c) a lean doesn't touch
♥.

## Decision

Trey: "a".

- Max Posture uses your ♥ points without the lean.
- A lean into ♥ acts at contact: it cuts the Posture damage you take on that hit. How much each
  leaned ♥ point cuts is a sim value.

## Consequences

- §4 *Stances* and *Stat value* say ♥ takes its lean at contact; contact resolution step 6
  subtracts the cut. The §11 stat rates row gains the ♥ lean cut.
- The stat meter (0037) shows ♥ with the lean applied, as the cut it would give on this hit.
