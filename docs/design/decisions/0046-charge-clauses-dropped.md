## Context

0028 gave the straight, The Charge, two clauses: its strike can't be Blocked, and its exposure
widens by one direction (positions 0–6). 0032 added the meter. Trey: "i just want straight
holders and potential straight holders to hold their aim." Neither clause makes a rider hold.
Can't be Blocked makes a late move cheaper, since a weak hit still lands, and the full-meter hit
already gets through a Block (0031). The widened exposure only makes an already pinned rider
easier to crit.

## Decision

Trey: "a yes".

- The Charge's strike can be Blocked, and its exposure is the standard one.
- Its out is the widest on the ladder in timing, not positions: normal exposure, and the rider
  can't move without resetting the meter.

## Consequences

- §6 effects table updated. The §11 straight exposure row carried by 0043 is removed.
- This replaces 0028's two Charge clauses; the rest of 0028 stands.
