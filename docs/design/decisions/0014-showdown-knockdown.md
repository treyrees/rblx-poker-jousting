## Context

Posture (0009) and Score were two tracks. Aiming on the axis that fed only Posture rarely
unhorsed and never gained Score, so it looked dominated. Trey asked whether Score could be
shed for an implicit score.

## Decision

Trey: "hand strength forces a knockdown, with posture input still mattering."

- There is no Score track. Every hit is Posture damage.
- If no one is unhorsed after Pass 4, a knockdown is forced at showdown. The rider who is
  weaker on hand strength and Posture together is knocked down.
- Showdown ties compare poker hands with kickers; still tied, split.

## Consequences

- §2 win conditions, §3, §4, §9 and §11 updated. §11's stale rows are replaced, as Trey
  asked ("replace everything stale"): the layout, armor, ♠, lean and showdown rows are new;
  the block leak row is removed.
- Pillar 1 says outcomes come from the joust, "not a card comparison, except as a final
  tiebreak." Hand strength now forces the showdown result. The pillar's wording is Trey's
  to revisit; it is unchanged here.
- Open: how hand strength converts, and its weight against Posture.
- §6 still names Score in several trick effects; a note marks them for the §6 review.
