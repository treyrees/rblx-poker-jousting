## Context

0078 sends a rider's prefold money into the pot of their next match as carry. It didn't say what
happens to the carry of a rider who leaves the yard before they keep a hand.

The options put to Trey:

- **It waits for them:** the carry stays with the rider and rides into their next match whenever
  they come back. Prefold money stays with the players, as 0078 intends, and leaving gets nothing
  back. Costs a small saved balance per player, and a pot can hold carry from an earlier session.
- **The house keeps it:** simple, but a sliver of carry turns back into a sink, and leaving costs
  money for nothing.
- **It's refunded:** ruled out. A rider could prefold until they like a hand, quit when they don't
  and get the price back, so folding would be free for anyone willing to leave, and the price that
  holds 75% kept (0079) would mean nothing.
- **It goes to the yard's next match:** brings back the lottery 0078 avoided.

## Decision

Trey chose **it waits for them**. Trey: "A, im tempted to say you must win a match to not lose your
carry but its a kid friendly mobile game so we can just let it be a convenient UX thing."

- A rider's carry stays with them when they leave the yard and rides into their next match, in a
  later session if need be. Leaving never refunds it and never forfeits it.

## Consequences

- §2 *The yard* says so.
- Nothing in the sim changes: the yard model has no sessions, and its price is for a rider carrying
  nothing.
