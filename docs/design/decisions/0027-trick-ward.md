## Context

Under 0020, tricks on different rungs both apply. Under 0026 both are built to unhorse, so
they would unhorse each other, and §2's mutual-unhorse rule (more Posture before the pass)
would decide the hand. The review first proposed a rule that a lower trick's hit can't
unhorse a higher rider; that settles the outcome by rule, against 0025. The review then
proposed a ward.

## Decision

Trey: "A trick ward would only ever 'be relevant' to make sure a lower trick loses to a
higher - thinking thats completely justified its 'prescriptive' nature."

- Unleashing or answering also wards the rider: armor that counts against trick hits only,
  never numeric ones.
- Trick hits grow explosively up the ladder. Each rung's ward stops the hits of every rung
  below it, but not its own rung's or higher.
- Across rungs, a higher trick's hit breaks the lower ward, and a lower trick's hit can't get
  through the higher one. The straight, the bottom rung, needs none.
- Ward sizes are sim values.

## Consequences

- §6 *Trick effects on the unleash pass* gains the ward.
- Level tricks clash (0019), so wards never meet at the same rung.
- The review suggested each ward could equal the hit of the rung below, so it needs no
  values of its own; the sim then keeps each hit big enough to unhorse after a lower ward's
  cut. Left to the sim.
