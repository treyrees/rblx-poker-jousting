## Context

Trey wants straight holders to hold their aim. The meter (0032) does this on the unleash pass,
but a straight held and not unleashed had a flat +3 passive, so nothing rewarded holding on the
passes before. The review offered a passive that grows with hold within each pass. Trey asked
whether it meant a charge built across passes; the review advised against that: extra power on
the unleash is wasted (a full meter already unhorses, and a straight can't break a higher ward,
0027), it rewards not unleashing (against 0018), and "charge" is already the ♠ store and the
straight's name.

## Decision

Trey: "within pass version."

- A held straight's passive, +3 to the top card's suit stat, grows with hold: 0 at the start of
  each pass, the full +3 at full hold. It resets each pass, since every pass starts at Neutral.
- The curve is a sim value.

## Consequences

- §6 effects table and the §11 held passive row updated. The +3 size is unchanged.
- Holding long becomes a mild straight tell, with a second plausible cause: any rider holding a
  value aim (§3).
- This closes the §6 review; its in-progress note is removed.
