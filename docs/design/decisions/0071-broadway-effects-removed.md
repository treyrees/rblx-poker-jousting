## Context

The pass drafted broadway effects twice. The first draft gave each rank an identity expressed four
ways by suit (a 4×4 grid). Trey: "too many effects added". The second was suit-blind: one effect
per rank sized by count, plus a pair unlock. Trey: "im no longer confident these broadway card
effects are justified in the grand scheme. too much going on to make them cool without them
straight up revealing your cards". A visible face effect is a tell with one cause, which §7's rule
(every tell has at least two plausible causes) can't allow.

Two remaining spaces were offered. Hole faces as private information: Trey, "I want to like hole
faces granting info but thats hard to capture, so mark it as a v2 feature." Board faces as arena
rules: Trey, "Not worth it. Board effects don't have the right 'player power' feel as something
they do if theyre in your hand … im stuck with admitting that court cards will be boring, for now."

## Decision

- J, Q, K and A have no effects in v1. They add their rank value to their suit's stat like any card
  (0036, 0039).
- All of §5's effects go: the single and board effects, and the pocket pairs JJ Masquerade, QQ Twin
  Favor, KK High Court and AA Champion (supersedes 0055 and 0056).
- Board faces are presentation only: their arena emblems (§8).
- Pairs and trips of faces are the numeric multipliers (§4), like any rank.
- Hole faces granting private information is a v2 idea.

## Consequences

- §5 is reduced to the rank-value rule. §3, §7, §11 and the Status table drop the face effects.
- The jack no longer randomizes its color, so a hole jack shows its true color (§7).
