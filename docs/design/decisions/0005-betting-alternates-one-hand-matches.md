## Context

§2 described simultaneous betting decisions. The §2 review also asked whether a match should be
one hand, or several hands played with chip stacks so that bets matter inside the match.

## Decision

- Trey: "betting isnt simultaneous, jousting is. betting alternates until a bet is agreed upon,
  then jousting happens for the agreed upon bet."
- The button rider acts first in every betting round. The button passes each hand.
- Actions are Stay (check or call), Raise and Yield, with one re-raise per round, a 5 s timer
  and a timeout that counts as Stay.
- A match stays one hand. Trey: "A makes for more portable games ... a match is a hand with the
  results. Bets dont matter inside the larger heads up metagame, but the game stays simple and
  portable."

## Consequences

- §2 drops the Turbo Jousting Shield line, and the street multipliers move out of *Betting rules*.
- The action timer joins the §11 parameter table.
- Trey named preflop folding the biggest question: with one hand per match, a preflop Yield
  ends the match before any joust. It is a §11 open question.
