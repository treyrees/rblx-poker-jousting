## Context

With the four stats defined (0010), stats needed a boundary with tricks, so that numeric hands
stay modest edges (pillar 2) and tricks stay trump cards (pillar 3).

## Decision

- Stats change what each outcome is worth. Tricks change which outcomes exist or where the
  sectors sit.
- A trips-loaded rider still loses to an unleashed trick, and a clean read still swings more
  than trips against high card.
- The flush is each stat made explosive, so numeric play teaches the tricks.

## Consequences

- Taken up when §6 (Open) is reworked. Broadway effects that change the table, such as AA's
  edge crits, are revisited in the §5 review.
