## Context

0078 gives both riders' carry to the match's winner. A match can also split (§2 win conditions 2
and 4), about 0.1–0.2% of matches in the sim. The bets are equal by then, but two riders can bring
different carry, so §2's "split the pot" needed a rule for the carry.

The options put to Trey:

- **Each carry rides on:** nobody won, so each rider's carry goes with them to their next match,
  as if this one hadn't happened. Fits carry's one job, being won, and matches 0085.
- **Each rider takes their own carry back:** the carry turns back into units, a small refund of the
  prefold price, the one way it would come back without a win.
- **Halve the whole pot, carry included:** the literal hold'em reading; the rider who brought less
  carry gains half the difference in a tie nobody won.

## Decision

Trey chose **each carry rides on**: "A".

- In a split the bets are split as before, and each rider's carry rides on to their next match.

## Consequences

- §2 *The yard* says so. 0078's last open edge closes.
- Nothing in the sim changes: its yard model prices carry at its average, and splits are too rare
  to move it.
