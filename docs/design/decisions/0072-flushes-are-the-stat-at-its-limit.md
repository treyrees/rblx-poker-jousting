## Context

0011 made the flush "each stat made explosive". With the stats redefined (0067–0070), the flush
rules were checked against them.

## Decision

Trey: "Yes, as proposed".

- ♠ **Needle:** every hit you land is a Crit that pierces all armor. (The charge clause goes with
  the charge, 0067.)
- ♦ **Gilded Mirror:** unchanged.
- ♣ **Shattering Blow:** your hit lands in full even into a Block, and the target stays Battered
  (0068) for the rest of the hand.
- ♥ **Unbroken:** your Posture can't fall below 1 this pass, and after contact you heal back
  everything the pass took.
- The flush stays strongly tied to its home aim (0059): Trey chose "Strongly, as now". The flush
  commits in space as the straight commits in time, and its predictability is the numeric rider's
  out.

## Consequences

- §6 *Flush by suit* and the trick table are updated.
