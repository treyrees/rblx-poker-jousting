## Context

0012 made aim lean into the stats it points at, leaving open which directions point at which
stats. Trey asked for two arrangements to be compared: "Compare blacks and reds next to each
other to allow all o or all d, as opposed to color matches being opposed."

- A, colors adjacent: Up ♣, Out ♠, Down ♥, In ♦. Up-Out leans all offense, Down-In all
  defense. Opposite aims clash rivals: ♠ piercing against ♦ armor, ♣ raw damage against ♥
  Posture. The ring's halves match the lance/shield colors.
- B, colors opposite: every diagonal mixes offense and defense, and a punish always leans the
  other color. Flatter stat edges, but the clash pits twins and there is no one-line rule.

## Decision

Trey: "Let's go A, and make sure being on a diagonal isnt net positive inherently."

- Up ♣, Out ♠, Down ♥, In ♦. A cardinal leans into one stat and a diagonal into two.
- The total lean is the same at every aim. A diagonal shares it evenly between its two stats;
  a half step shares it mostly toward the nearer cardinal. Neutral leans into no stat.

## Consequences

- §4 *Stances* gains the compass; §3 Neutral and §1 Status updated.
- Buff size and the half-step share are §11 sim values. The sim checks that Up-Out and
  Down-In don't beat every cardinal, and that two-suit loading doesn't outgrow pillar 2's
  "modest stat edges".
