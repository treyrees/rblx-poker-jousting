## Context

0012 made aim lean into the stats it points at, leaving open which directions point at which
stats. The agent proposed a compass on the old home directions (Up ♣, Out ♠, Down ♥, In ♦), a
cardinal leaning into one stat and a diagonal into two.

## Decision

Trey: "Compare blacks and reds next to each other to allow all o or all d, as opposed to color
matches being opposed."

- Up ♣, Out ♠, Down ♥, In ♦. The black suits are adjacent and so are the red suits.
- A cardinal leans into one stat and a diagonal into two: Up-Out is all offense, Down-In all
  defense, Up-In ♣ ♦, Out-Down ♠ ♥.
- A half step leans mostly toward the nearer cardinal. Neutral leans into no stat.

## Consequences

- §4 *Stances* gains the compass; §3 Neutral and §1 Status updated.
- Buff size and the split on diagonals and half steps are §11 sim values.
