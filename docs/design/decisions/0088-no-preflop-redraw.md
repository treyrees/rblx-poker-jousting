## Context

0006 kept "a preflop redraw for a price" as open design space, and §11 asked: "a second
hand-selection tool alongside the prefold. What can be redrawn, and what does it cost?"

With the prefold settled (0078–0080), a redraw of both cards in the yard would be the prefold
itself. The shapes put to Trey that would differ from it:

- **One card:** keep one hole card and swap the other, a finer, cheaper tool than the all-or-nothing
  prefold.
- **After matching:** a public redraw in the match, before Bet 1, that works as a tell (§7) as well
  as a choice.

## Decision

Trey: "i don't think either of these are justified to keep when normal flopping exists, so lets
move on."

- There is no preflop redraw. The prefold is the one hand-selection tool in the yard.

## Consequences

- §1 Status drops the redraw row; §11 drops the redraw question.
- 0006's "kept as open design space" is closed by this decision.
- Nothing in the sim changes; it never modelled a redraw.
