## Context

The review offered a trump rule (the higher trick applies and the lower one fizzles) or the
old rule, both effects apply and the dial resolves them, with each trick designed to beat
the ones below it. Under 0018 two tricks built to unhorse can unhorse each other, and some
pairs of rules contradict outright (an unblockable strike against a Guard wall).

## Decision

Trey: "6.3 both apply, lets do our best." On a fallback where two rules contradict: "yes
that fallback is ok. a good thing not to get caught up on in designing the game".

- Tricks on different rungs both apply, and the dial resolves them.
- Each trick is designed to beat the tricks below it on the dial.
- Where two tricks' rules contradict outright, the higher trick's rule wins that
  contradiction; everything else still applies.

## Consequences

- §6 *Trick vs trick* rewritten.
- The effect rebuild gives each rung a piece that answers the rungs below it. The sim checks
  that a higher trick wins across rungs.
