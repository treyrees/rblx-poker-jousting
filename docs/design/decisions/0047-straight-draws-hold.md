## Context

Trey wants riders drawing to a straight to hold their aim too. 0032 already does this: the meter
counts hold from the start of the charge, and the card lands at 3.0 s, so a rider who wants to
unleash on the pass the straight lands must already be holding. The review advised against a
mechanic that rewards draws themselves: it would need draw detection and would leak draws.

## Decision

Trey: "c i like that recommendation".

- *The straight's meter* says it outright: a rider drawing to a straight who wants to unleash on
  the pass it lands must hold from the start of the charge. No new rule.

## Consequences

- §6 *The straight's meter* gains the line.
