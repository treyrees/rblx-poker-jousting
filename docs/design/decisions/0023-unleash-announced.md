## Context

Unleashes were hidden until contact, except the flush, which revealed its suit so the
opponent could Guard its axis. Axes are gone (0012, 0016), and pillar 3 no longer wants
that kind of counterplay.

## Decision

Trey: "6.6 recommended."

- Declaring announces the trick's rung to both riders. A flush also shows its suit.
- The trick's cards stay hidden until contact.
- A held trick that answers (0021) is not announced; it shows at contact.

## Consequences

- §6 *Unleash rules* updated.
