## Context

*Board-made tricks* predated the §6 review. Open Lists and Siege named Score, which 0014
removed; board quads had a Score tie rule; the board flush added +8 to a stat "toward its
axis". Since 0034 stats are card points (a hole ace is 3), so +8 would outweigh two hole aces,
and a board flush's five cards already count for both riders at board weight. As written,
"all output ×0.5" would also halve a trick's hit over a board full house, which could stop it
unhorsing (0018).

## Decision

Trey: "r3.1 recommended. numbers can come later".

- Open Lists (board straight): numeric Posture damage ×1.5. The Score clause is dropped.
- Board flush: an arena moment in the suit's colors, with no stat bonus.
- Siege (board full house): numeric output ×0.5; the hand leans on the showdown knockdown.
- Board quads: an arena moment with no rule of its own; kickers settle a showdown tie as usual.
- Board straight flush: board flush + Open Lists, kept.
- The arena multipliers scale numeric hits only; a trick's hit keeps its size.
- The ×1.5 and ×0.5 are placeholders. Their numbers come later; they are not moved into §11
  yet.

## Consequences

- §6 *Board-made tricks* rewritten.
