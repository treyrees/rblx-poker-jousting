## Context

§6 said a board trick is board-made only if neither rider improves on it. If one did, the
improver owned a trick and the other owned none, so the improver was above and could unleash
to win the hand, often on a trivial improvement: a 3♥ over a 2♥ board flush, a 10 on a 5–9
board straight, or a hole card as the kicker to board quads. A deal sim (200k deals) found that
when the board makes a trick, one rider improves within its rung about 28% of the time and to
a higher rung about 5%.

## Decision

Trey: "r3.2 recommended".

- When the board alone makes a trick, both riders stand on its rung. A rider playing the
  board's trick can't unleash it, and it doesn't fire on Pass 4.
- If the opponent unleashes a trick on that rung, the board's trick answers and they clash
  (0019).
- Only hole cards that lift a rider to a higher rung put them above. An improvement within the
  rung is level; the stronger hand counts at the showdown knockdown.
- The arena effect is on whenever the board alone makes a trick, whether or not a rider
  improves on it.

## Consequences

- §6 *The trick ladder* and *Ownership* updated.
- Both riders are at least on the board's rung, so a board trick never faces a lower unleash
  and never needs a ward.
- A rider above the other on a board trick drops from about 33% of board tricks to about 5%.
