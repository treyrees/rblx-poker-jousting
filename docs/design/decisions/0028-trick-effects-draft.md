## Context

The old effects used notches, axes and Score, and were built for counterplay under the old
pillar 3. The review proposed a ladder in which each trick's dial change sets its out: the
narrow way a numeric rider can still survive, by unhorsing the trick rider on the same
contact while ahead on Posture before the pass (§2, win condition 2). The out narrows as the
ladder climbs.

## Decision

Trey: "the ladder is an excellent starting draft."

- Straight, The Charge: can't be Blocked; exposure widens one direction (positions 0–6).
- Flush, Suit Ascendant: the flush suit's stat explodes, full at its compass home and half on
  the diagonals beside it. ♣ Shattering Blow lands in full even into a Block; ♠ Needle makes
  every landed hit a Crit that pierces all armor and spends charge.
- Full house, Fortress: Guard on every position but one secret gap direction; a hit on the
  gap is a Crit.
- Quads, Four Lances: the strike lands on all four cardinals, so one always crits; the lances
  parry the opponent's strike.
- Straight flush: The Charge and Suit Ascendant with no exposure. The royal: the same, with
  its own presentation.
- The held passives are kept (0024). The counterplay column becomes "Out left to a numeric
  rider".

## Consequences

- §6 *Trick effects on the unleash pass* and *Flush by suit* rewritten. §1 Status: trick
  effects move from Open to Proposed, needs sim.
- The §11 trick rows (×1.5 and 4 directions, ×2, 7 of 8, 50%) predate this draft. They are
  left as they are until Trey updates them.
- The ♦ flush is named Gilded Ward, close to the ward of 0027; a rename is Trey's call.
