## Context

0010 kept the old stance rule: offense stats follow the aim's axis, defense stats don't
follow aim. That left aim choosing a weapon, or nothing at all.

## Decision

Trey: "i think all stats do something all the time, but aim towards one/two stats buff the
impact those stats have on the interaction ... so not only is it the relative distance of
yours and opponents dials, but the absolute position relative to axes which stats get buffed
further."

- All four stats act in every exchange.
- Aim leans into the stats it points at, buffing their impact.
- The distance between the two aims picks the outcome row; each aim's absolute position picks
  its lean.

## Consequences

- §4 *Stances* is rewritten. The axis rule, the Posture/Score routing by axis and the
  diagonal 50/50 split are gone.
- Open: which directions lean into which stats (including half steps and Neutral), and the
  size of the buff.
