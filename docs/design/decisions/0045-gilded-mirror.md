## Context

0028 named the ♦ flush Gilded Ward, close to the ward of 0027. The review offered renaming the
flush, renaming the ward, or keeping both.

## Decision

Trey: "rename the flush", and "gilded mirror".

- The ♦ flush is Gilded Mirror. Its effect is unchanged: armor over the whole dial, and what it
  stops reflects back at the striker.

## Consequences

- §6 *Flush by suit* and §8's counter list updated. 0028 and 0029 keep the old name as records.
