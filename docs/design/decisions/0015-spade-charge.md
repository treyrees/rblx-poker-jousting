## Context

0010 made ♠ the crit, armor-piercing and pay-later stat, with Trey's proposal that non-crits
buff later crits. The agent proposed details.

## Decision

Trey: "go with spade idea."

- Your Normal and Block hits pierce armor by ♠ and add to your charge.
- Your next crit, including a crossed-lances crit (CC), spends all of it as a bonus.
- Charge lasts the whole hand, so early-pass charge pays at later street multipliers.
- The outcomes that build charge are public; the amount is hidden.
