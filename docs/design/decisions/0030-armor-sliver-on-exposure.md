## Context

♦ armor was thick on the Guard, thin on ordinary positions and absent on the exposure, so ♦
did nothing against a crit and ♠ piercing did nothing on one. Trey asked: "is it good to give
everywhere a small amount of armor with it much higher at the guard (conceptually) so armor
pierce has nominal value everwhere?"

## Decision

Trey: "yes, sliver on exposure, to allow armorpierce to always have value."

- ♦ armor: thick on the Guard, thin on ordinary positions, a sliver on the exposure.
- ♠ pierces armor on every hit, crits included. Normal and Block hits still build charge;
  crits still scale by ♠ and spend it.

## Consequences

- §4 *The four stats* and contact resolution steps 4 and 5 updated. ♦ now acts on every hit,
  which fits pillar 5 ("all four act all the time").
- The §11 armor row reads Thick / thin / sliver; values are still set by the sim.
