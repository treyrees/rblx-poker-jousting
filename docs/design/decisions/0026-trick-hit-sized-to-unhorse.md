## Context

0018 builds an unleashed trick above the opponent to unhorse them on that pass. The old trick
numbers (×1.5, ×2) come nowhere near that: a trips-loaded Normal on Pass 3 does about 22
Posture against 100 or more, and Pass 1's street multiplier is ×0.5.

## Decision

Trey: "without saying too much for certain, it does feel like a trick should have enough to
make sure the round ends with the opponent unhorsed."

- An unleashed trick's hit is sized to unhorse the opponent from full Posture on any pass,
  Pass 1 included. Its size is a sim value.

## Consequences

- Armor is a flat reduction per hit, so a hit that size gets through any sector, a Block
  included. What each trick changes on the dial then sets its out rather than whether it wins
  (0028).
- How the hit clears Pass 1's street multiplier is left to the sim.
