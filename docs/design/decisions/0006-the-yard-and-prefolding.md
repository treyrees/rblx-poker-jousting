## Context

With one hand per match (0005), a preflop Yield ends the match before any joust, so hold'em's
preflop fold had no place. Trey called this the biggest question.

## Decision

- Trey: "its easy to visualize riders circling a yard, and once two riders have a hand they dont
  want to prefold ... they step forward and meet their 'match' (matchmade behind the scenes).
  betting ensues. it can be diegetic."
- Riders are dealt hole cards in the yard. Prefolding costs something and deals a new hand.
  Riders who keep their hands are matched behind the scenes.
- Trey: "grading in units won was always correct." A match's result is the units won or lost.
- A preflop redraw for a price is kept as open design space. A fold that doesn't end the match
  (half stakes or reduced stats) is not pursued.

## Consequences

- §2 gains *The yard*. The preflop-folding open question is replaced by the prefold cost and
  the redraw.
- Status: the yard is Settled in shape; the redraw is Open; the economy row narrows to currency,
  wager limits and monetization.
- The §9 tilt-yard lobby row is revisited in the §9 review.
