## Context

0067 has ♠ make crits "easier+better" and ♦ make blocks "easier+better", more when aimed. Trey
asked to explore three levers: less hold needed, wider zones, and extra time to aim past the
deadline.

## Decision

Trey: "i like your easier crits and blocks suggestion":

- **Always, scaled by the stat:** less hold needed. A crit reaches full power with less hold, so a
  late read onto the exposure keeps its punch; the Guard reaches full armor with less hold.
- **When aimed at the stat:** extra time past the aim lock, sized by the lean. When both riders
  lean into ♠ or ♦, their extra times offset.
- **Better:** crits hit bigger with ♠; blocks hold more with ♦.
- **Wider zones** were left to the face cards, which 0071 then removed, so no stat widens a zone.

## Consequences

- §3 *Aim lock* and §4 describe them. The hold relief and the extra time are sim values (§11).
