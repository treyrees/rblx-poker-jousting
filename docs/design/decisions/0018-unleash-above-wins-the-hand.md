## Context

Pillar 3 (0002) gives a rider above the opponent on the ladder who unleashes a virtually
guaranteed win condition. §6 still said a trick "does not win outright". The review offered
three readings of what an unleash wins: A, the hand, on that pass (the trick is built to
unhorse); B, a decisive swing, with the hand going on; C, an effect that lasts the rest of
the hand.

## Decision

Trey: "6.1 A".

- An unleashed trick above the opponent is built to unhorse them on that pass, so it wins
  the hand.
- Unleashing ends the hand before the opponent can draw level or above, for the pot bet so
  far. Holding builds the pot and hides the trick, at the risk of a draw-out and of being
  worn down while riding as the sub-hand.

## Consequences

- §6 intro rewritten and *The trick ladder* added.
- Trick numbers must be able to unhorse even at Pass 1's street multiplier. The effect
  rebuild, and what is left of "virtually", are the review's next round.
- The §6 frequency text now gives owned-trick rates and the street split from a deal sim
  (Trey agreed to the update under 6.7, recorded in 0024).
