## Context

§11 asked whether the unhorse should be a visible-odds roll at low Posture (Turbo Jousting's
teeter). Since 0053 a roll could only act on Passes 1–3, which end about 11% of hands, the endings
where a read or a trick has just won. §9 already listed the teeter as replaced by deterministic
Posture.

## Decision

Trey chose "No: hard 0". Posture stays deterministic.

## Consequences

- The §11 open question is removed, and §9's teeter row no longer says it could return.
