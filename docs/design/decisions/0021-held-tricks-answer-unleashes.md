## Context

§8 calls a held trick countering an unleashed one "the biggest moment in the game", but §6
had no rule that let a held trick counter. The review asked whether a held trick above an
unleash answers it. It also found that if a held level trick could only answer by hand, an
unleash declared just before aim lock could not be answered in time, so level tricks would
be decided by declaration timing.

## Decision

Trey: "6.4 yes". On declaration timing: "gap 2 seems easy to fix".

- A held trick answers an unleash from its own rung or below: it fires automatically on the
  same pass, however late the unleash is declared. The answer is its unleash for the hand.
- Answering from above: both apply (0020). Answering from the same rung: the tricks clash
  (0019).
- A held trick below the unleash doesn't answer; it rides as its sub-hand.

## Consequences

- §6 *Held behavior* and *Trick vs trick* updated. §8's held-counter moment now has a rule.
