## Context

Trey opened a design pass on the stats, the broadway cards and the tricks: "Design via concept,
fantasy, game and skill expression is more important than any existing balance findings." The
working record is [passes/stats-and-broadway.md](../passes/stats-and-broadway.md).

## Decision

Trey: "strength increases damage, moreso when you aim … accuracy increases armor penetration
always, makes critting easier+better in ways, moreso when you aim. armor increases armor reduction
always, makes blocking easier+better in ways, moreso when you aim. posture increases health, aiming
gives health before contact, but heals after contact always."

- The stats are **♣ Strength**, **♠ Accuracy**, **♦ Armor** and **♥ Posture** (formerly Knockoff,
  Pierce, Armor and Sturdiness).
- Each offense stat mirrors a defense stat: Strength ↔ Posture (damage vs health), Accuracy ↔
  Armor (penetration vs reduction, crits vs blocks).
- **♠'s pay-later charge is dropped** (supersedes 0015). ♣ owns "later" through its condition
  (0068).
- What each stat does is in 0068 (♣), 0069 (♠ and ♦) and 0070 (♥).

## Consequences

- §4 *The four stats*, *Stances*, *Stat value* and *Contact resolution*, and the §11 rate rows, are
  rewritten.
- The raw/circumstantial pairing (0010) still holds: Strength and Posture are raw; Accuracy and
  Armor are circumstantial.
