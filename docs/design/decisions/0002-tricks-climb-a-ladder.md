## Context

Pillar 3 read "Tricks are trump cards, not auto-wins": each trick traded power for
predictability so reads could still beat it, and trick vs read was an intended arms race.

## Decision

Trey: "Tricks should result in unique mechanics and/or explosively scaled numbers as the trick
hierarchy climbs to offer virtually guaranteed win conditions when you are above the opponent on
the trick ladder (and you deploy your trick rather than hiding it)."

Pillar 3 now reads: **Tricks are trump cards.** Straight and above are tricks, ranked on a trick
ladder. As the ladder climbs, tricks bring unique mechanics and/or explosively scaled numbers. A
rider above the opponent on the ladder who unleashes their trick, rather than holding it, has a
virtually guaranteed win condition.

## Consequences

- The §1 Status row for tricks is Open until §6 is reworked to this pillar.
- Text that still assumes the old pillar, to revisit in its section's review: §6 intro and
  counterplay column, §9 "Reads always beat rarity" row, §11 trick win-rate open question
  (75 to 90%).
