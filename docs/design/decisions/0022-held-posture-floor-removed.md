## Context

Posture could not drop below 1 while a rider held a trick. Under 0018 and auto-fire on Pass
4, that made slow-playing free against numeric hands, and it would have kept a lower held
trick alive against a higher unleash, breaking pillar 3. It was a §11 open question.

## Decision

Trey: "6.5 remove on your recommendation".

- No Posture floor. A rider holding a trick can be unhorsed like any other.

## Consequences

- §6 *Held behavior* updated. The §11 open question on the floor is answered and removed.
- §7 loses the "Refusing to fall" tell.
- Holding now costs something: the trick rides as its sub-hand while the opponent can wear
  its Posture down.
