## Context

GAME_SPEC lists what is public (§3) and the cues (§7), not where they sit on screen. Put to Trey,
for a UI-only, mobile-first slice (0103): bars top, cards middle, wheel bottom; a side-by-side
split; or the wheel in the center.

## Decision

- **Top:** the opponent's row (nameplate, Posture bar in two rows of 8 hearts, hold meter, aim),
  then the player's row.
- **Middle:** the pass clock, the board and the player's hole cards, the pot.
- **Bottom:** the Magnet wheel with the opponent's sectors projected on the ring (§3), the stat
  meter beside it (§4), and both riders' lance and shield colors (§7).
- **Bets:** Stay / Raise / Yield over the wheel between passes, with the 5 s timer.
- **Beats:** the reveal and the heal are banners over the cards.

## Consequences

- The wheel shows Up at the top, sweeping clockwise, so Out is on the right and In on the left.
