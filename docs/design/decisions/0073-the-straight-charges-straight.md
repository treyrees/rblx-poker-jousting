## Context

RESULTS.md found that an unheld straight wins about as often as a held one (98.2% vs 98.8%). The
meter's y is the top card's rank, so a high straight unhorses after a short hold.

## Decision

Trey: "The innate, wordless 'feel' of the straight to me is 'straightly' charging without changing
aim. i don't care how the math works."

- The straight's identity is holding one aim through the charge. That is where its power comes
  from; the sim sets the meter's math to match.

## Consequences

- §6 *The straight's meter* says so. Making an unheld straight weak is the sim's job (§11).
