## Context

0018 builds an unleashed trick above the opponent to unhorse them. The review's first
proposal for tricks on different rungs, that a lower trick's hit can't unhorse a higher
rider, was a rule settling the outcome.

## Decision

Trey: "victory needs to always be achieved by the system its never declared outright, i just
plan on that happening overwhelmingly when there is a trick in a better category."

- No rule declares a winner. The joust produces the result.
- A trick in a higher category is built to win overwhelmingly, through its numbers and
  mechanics.

## Consequences

- §6 intro says the win comes through the joust.
- The review's rule-based proposal for tricks on different rungs is replaced by the ward
  (0027).
- Trey also confirmed 0019's reading: "tricks of the same tier means the trump card goes away
  and the numbers/skill shine through."
