## Context

Defense doesn't unhorse on its own. The review asked whether the ♥ and ♦ flushes close the
numeric rider's out, or keep the same normal-exposure out as ♣ and ♠.

## Decision

Trey: "defense: Recommended: they close the out".

- ♥ Unbroken: you can't be unhorsed this pass.
- ♦ Gilded Ward: your armor covers your whole dial, exposure included, and what it stops
  reflects back at the striker.

## Consequences

- §6 *Flush by suit* updated. ♥ and ♦ flushes are safer than ♣ and ♠ flushes against a
  numeric rider; defense wins safe.
- Against a higher trick, a contradiction with Unbroken goes to the higher trick (0020).
